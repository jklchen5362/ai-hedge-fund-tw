常見問題與解答 (FAQ) Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1: 如何開始使用 AI 評估股票？ Q1: How do I begin AI stock analysis?

開啟 App「分析」首頁，在輸入框輸入台股 4 碼代號（如 2330 或 2330.TW）或美股代號（如 NVDA、AAPL），點擊「新增」後按下「開始評估」，系統即會在數秒內並行調用 AI 矩陣計算交易評級與多空分數。

Open the "Analysis" tab in the App, enter a 4-digit TW stock ticker (e.g. 2330 or 2330.TW) or US ticker (e.g. NVDA, AAPL), tap "Add", and then tap "Run Multi-Agent Evaluation". The multi-agent AI matrix will compute ratings and sentiment scores in parallel within seconds.

Q2: 如何恢復 Pro 會員購買 (Restore Purchases)？ Q2: How do I restore my Pro subscription?

若您更換新裝置或重新安裝 App，請在 App 內點擊「升級 Pro」看板，並點擊下方「恢復購買」按鈕；或至「系統設定」>「恢復已購買權限」，系統將透過您的 Apple ID 自動恢復權益。

If you switch devices or reinstall the app, tap "Upgrade to Pro" in the app and select "Restore Purchases" at the bottom, or navigate to "Settings" > "Restore Purchases". Your entitlement will be restored via your Apple ID.

Q3: 如何管理或取消自動續訂訂閱？ Q3: How do I manage or cancel auto-renewable subscriptions?

您可以隨時透過 iOS 裝置的「設定」>「頂部您的 Apple ID」>「訂閱項目」中找到「台股智投 Pro」，點選即可變更方案或取消訂閱。請注意需於目前計費週期結束前至少 24 小時前取消。

You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime via iOS "Settings" > "[Your Apple ID]" > "Subscriptions" > "AgentFund AI Pro". Cancellations must be made at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing cycle.

Q4: 年費方案是否支援家人共享？ Q4: Does the Annual Plan support Apple Family Sharing?

是的，「台股智投 Pro 年費方案」支援 Apple 家人共享功能。您只需訂閱年費，即可與您「家人共享」群組中的最多 5 位家庭成員共同使用完整 Pro 功能。

Yes! The AgentFund AI Pro Annual Plan supports Apple Family Sharing. A single subscription can be shared with up to 5 family members in your Family Sharing group.

Q5: 數據分析是否需要額外付費設定 API Key？ Q5: Do I need to provide external API keys or pay extra?

不需要！本 App 內建全套量化規則與本地端多代理人模型，無需提供任何 API 金鑰即可完整運算。若進階使用者想接入自己的 OpenAI 或 Gemini 模型，亦可於「系統設定」中自由輸入。

No! The App includes a built-in quantitative rule engine and local multi-agent model that operates out of the box with zero setup. Advanced users who wish to connect their own OpenAI or Gemini API keys can optionally do so in App Settings.
